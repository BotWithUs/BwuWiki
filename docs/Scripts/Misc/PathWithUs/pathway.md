## Overview

:::::

<ContentBlock title="About the Predefined Pathway">

> PathWithUs ships with a **predefined pathway** — a single ordered queue of **299 steps** that takes a fresh account from its first backpack of shrimps all the way through Necromancy tier 20 gear, Fort Forinthry, and a long chain of quests.
>
> - The queue runs **strictly top to bottom**. Step 1 runs first, step 299 runs last.
> - Every step checks whether it is **already done** before it runs. If your account already has the level, the item, or the quest completed, the step is skipped automatically — so you can start the pathway on an account that is already partway through.
> - The pathway is a **starting point, not a contract**. You can remove steps, reorder them, or add your own from the Add Task menu at any time.
> - Ordering matters in a few places. Several banking steps exist purely to clear the backpack before the step that follows, and a handful of steps are deliberately positioned so their outputs are in the bank when a later step needs them. If you reorder, keep the banking step next to the step it serves.
> - Steps are numbered **1 to 299** continuously on this page, so you can say "I'm on step 147" and mean exactly one thing.

</ContentBlock>

<ContentBlock title="How to read the tables">

> - **#** is the position in the queue.
> - **Task** is a shortened label for the step, not the exact line the queue window shows. Quest steps in particular are listed by quest name rather than by their full "Complete *X* quest" description.
> - **What it does** is the plain-English summary.
>
> Section headings match the stage comments in the pathway itself, so the grouping here is the same grouping the script uses internally.

</ContentBlock>

## Not in this pathway

<ContentBlock title="Catalog-only tasks">

> Not everything PathWithUs can do is in the pathway above.
>
> - **46 standalone quests** are registered as **catalog-only**. They are available from the Add Task menu and from the "Load quests only" option, but they are deliberately kept out of the predefined pathway so they can be queued one at a time rather than chained together. Several of them need specific items in the backpack or bank before you start, and some have manual steps.
> - **Bank presets 1 to 18** are also catalog-only. Each one teleports to the Burthorpe lodestone, opens the bank and loads that preset, so you can drop one between quests to swap gear and supplies mid-chain.
>
> For the full list of catalog-only quests, their requirements, and which ones have manual steps, see [Quest Coverage](./quests.md).

</ContentBlock>
